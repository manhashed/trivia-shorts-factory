## Design Context

### Users
Kids entertainment creators making short-form trivia videos for children ages 5–8 (early elementary). They need to move quickly from question ideas to polished, repeatable shorts that invite kids to shout answers out loud.

### Brand Personality
Whimsical, warm, premium. The studio should feel focused and capable; the generated videos should feel like a tiny storybook game show with a friendly host.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the creator studio dark and premium, with restrained amber, mint, coral, and sky accents. Generated videos should use storybook motion, character-led transitions, musical punctuation, readable type, and a clear participation rhythm. Avoid generic neon dashboards, noisy effects, and decoration that competes with the question.

### Design Principles
- Make every short ask for a response before revealing the answer.
- Use motion to clarify the story beat: ask, think, reveal, celebrate.
- Give voice, music, and sound effects separate controls with sensible defaults.
- Keep the question and answer readable on a phone at a glance.
- Respect reduced motion and keep audio opt-in at the final playback surface.
